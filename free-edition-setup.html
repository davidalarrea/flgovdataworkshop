The setup

Five steps to your free workspace

Databricks creates and manages the workspace for you. There's nothing to configure and nothing to wait on — each person gets their own.

01

Open the sign-up page

Go to the Databricks Free Edition sign-up page:

databricks.com/learn/free-edition

02

Choose how you sign in

Sign up with a Google account (fastest — one click) or with any email address, which sends you a one-time passcode.

03

Verify your account

Approve the Google prompt, or enter the one-time passcode emailed to you. Free Edition doesn't support corporate SSO — email or Google only.

04

Let Databricks build your workspace

It's created automatically on serverless compute. There are no clusters to size and no settings to pick.

05

You land in your workspace

That's it — you're in and ready. Keep the login you used; you'll sign back in with it on the day.

✓

Good to know

A few things worth knowing

Which email
Use a personal email or one that can receive an external one-time passcode. Some government mail systems block those — a personal Gmail is the easy fallback and enables one-click sign-in.
One per account
Free Edition gives you a single workspace, so you only need to sign up once.
Languages
Python and SQL are fully supported. R and Scala are not available on Free Edition — we won't need them.
Compute
Everything runs on serverless, so there are no clusters to start or manage.
What you can build
Notebooks, SQL, Genie Spaces, and AI/BI dashboards — exactly what we'll do together.
Optional: LinkedIn
Verifying LinkedIn in-product unlocks limited GPU and broader internet access. Not needed for our session unless your host says otherwise.

One ground rule

Free Edition is for learning and non-commercial use. Please keep exercises on the sample data we provide — don't load your agency's production or sensitive data into it.
